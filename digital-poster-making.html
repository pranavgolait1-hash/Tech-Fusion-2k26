Working / Proposed Event Rules

These event-specific points are a working draft until the official rules are issued by the event coordinators.

  • The poster must be original work created by the participating team.
  • Participants must follow the size, format and submission instructions announced by the coordinators.
  • Plagiarized or copied designs may be disqualified.
  • Participants should keep the original/editable source file if requested.
  • The work must be submitted within the time limit communicated by the coordinators.
