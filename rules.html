RULES & REGULATIONS

Common guidelines applicable across the competitions. Event-specific working rules are shown on each event page.

Common Rules

  • Only Polytechnic / Diploma students are eligible to participate.
  • Engineering degree students are not allowed.
  • Minimum 1 and maximum 4 members are permitted in a team.
  • College / Student ID card is compulsory for participants.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Maintain discipline and professional conduct throughout the event.
  • Judges' and coordinators' decisions will be final.
  • All submitted work must be original. Plagiarism may lead to disqualification.
  • One registration per participant/event unless otherwise permitted by the organizers.
  • Participants must follow event-specific time limits and submission instructions.
  • Participants should bring required technical materials/files as communicated for their event.
  • Late arrival may lead to cancellation of participation at the coordinators' discretion.
  • Organizers may modify rules when necessary and will communicate changes to participants.
