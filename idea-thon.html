IDEATHON – RULES & REGULATIONS

Official rules for Ideathon. Coordinators may share additional instructions during the event.

  1. Eligibility: Only currently enrolled Diploma/Polytechnic students are eligible to participate.
  2. Team Size: Each team shall consist of 1–4 members. A participant can be part of only one team.
  3. Ideathon Themes: Participants can submit innovative solutions under any one of the following themes:
    • Sustainability, Environment & Clean Energy
    • Agriculture & Rural Innovation
    • Healthcare & Well-being
    • Smart Infrastructure & Mobility
    • Smart Technology, AI & IoT
    • Open Innovation
  4. Originality: The idea must be original and innovative. Plagiarism or direct copying is strictly prohibited.
  5. Problem Statement: The idea must address a clearly identified real-world problem and provide a practical solution.
  6. Prototype: A working prototype or model is encouraged wherever applicable.
  7. Code of Conduct: Participants must maintain professional and respectful conduct throughout the event.
  8. Jury Decision: The jury's decision shall be final and binding.
  9. Rule Changes: The organizers may make necessary changes to the rules or schedule when required.
