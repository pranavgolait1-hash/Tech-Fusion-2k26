Idea Thon

Turn real-world problems into practical, innovative and impactful technology-driven ideas.

Event Information

  • Eligibility: Polytechnic / Diploma students only
  • Engineering degree students are not eligible
  • Team size: Minimum 1, Maximum 2 members
  • Registration: Individual ₹50 / Team ₹200
  • College / Student ID card is compulsory

Coordinator

  • Faculty Coordinator: Neha Jiwane — 7558320286

  • Follow the instructions communicated by the coordinators before and during the event.

Working / Proposed Event Rules

These event-specific points are a working draft until the official rules are issued by the event coordinators.

  • The idea should address a genuine real-world problem.
  • Teams should clearly explain the problem, proposed solution, innovation and expected impact.
  • Feasibility, usefulness and originality may be considered during evaluation.
  • Prototype/demo material may be shown if applicable and available.
  • Participants must follow the presentation time communicated by the coordinators.
